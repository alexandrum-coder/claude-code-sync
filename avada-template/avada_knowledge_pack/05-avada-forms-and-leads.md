# 05 - Avada Forms and Lead Generation

## Scop
Foloseste Avada Form pentru formulare native de contact si lead generation, inainte de a recomanda pluginuri externe.

## Tipuri de formulare

### Formular contact simplu
Campuri:
- Name
- Email
- Phone optional
- Message
- Consent checkbox daca este necesar legal

Unde se pune:
- Contact page
- CTA final
- Modal optional

### Formular request quote
Campuri:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Company
- Service needed
- Budget range optional
- Timeline optional
- Message

### Formular booking / consultanta
Campuri:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Preferred date/time
- Service interest
- Message

### Formular lead magnet
Campuri:
- Name optional
- Email
- Consent checkbox

## Reguli Claude
- Propune intai Avada Form.
- Specifica campurile, tipul campului, required/optional.
- Specifica mesajul de confirmare.
- Specifica notificarea email catre admin.
- Specifica redirect daca este cazul.
- Nu recomanda Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms sau WPForms decat daca cerinta depaseste Avada Forms.
